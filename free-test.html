    Technical details
    Free: the browser does the recognition itself — Chrome and Android use Google’s free speech service, Safari uses Apple’s, Edge uses Microsoft’s, so the voice is sent to that company (no key or account involved). Nothing is stored by this page except your text, in this browser. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari; Firefox has no built-in speech recognition.
